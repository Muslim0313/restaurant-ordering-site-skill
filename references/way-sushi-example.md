# Way Sushi example

The public [Way Sushi site](https://waysushi-germenchik.ru/) grew through owner feedback on a food ordering menu. The owner supplied a complete price list, exact delivery settlements, a pickup location, a WhatsApp number, and many individual food references. The design evolved from a generic menu to a purple branded page with a large wordmark, category-specific backgrounds, and product cards.

The key corrections were about fidelity: different rolls needed distinct images; new dishes had to match the category's established photo treatment; prices changed for specified items while other prices stayed as they were. Some names appeared in more than one category, so category and stable product ID mattered. The owner also requested a rotating featured dish, enlarged photos on tap, a mystery image for the house pizza, and a variable-price grilled chicken.

The site offers delivery and pickup, while the final order is sent by the customer through WhatsApp. The live version was deployed from a GitHub repository to Timeweb App Platform and checked at its HTTPS domain after the build. This example illustrates the workflow; do not copy its menu, pricing, contact details, host, or visual identity into another restaurant's site without that owner's request.
